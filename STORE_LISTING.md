## Detailed description

DevNest replaces Chrome's New Tab page with a dashboard built for software
engineers — everything you check first thing, in one place instead of six
tabs.

**Plan your day**
Full task management with priorities, due dates and times, and Today /
Upcoming / Overdue / Completed views. Reminders fire even if the New Tab
page isn't open.

**Get to your tools fast**
Pin the sites you open every day — GitHub, your ticket tracker, docs,
cloud console — organized by category, with automatic favicons.

**Time your work**
A timestamp-accurate Stopwatch, a configurable Timer with presets, and a
full Pomodoro mode with focus/break cycling and session tracking.

**18 developer utilities, one click away**
JSON formatting, Base64 and URL encode/decode, UUID generation, a
timestamp converter, hash generator, regex tester, JWT decoder, color
converter, case converter, Lorem Ipsum, random strings, a cron helper, a
SQL formatter, and searchable Git / Docker / Linux / HTTP-status
reference sheets — each opens in its own lightweight panel.

**Snippets and notes**
Save the commands you always have to look up, and jot down quick notes,
without leaving your new tab.

**See your day at a glance**
A Productivity widget shows tasks completed today and this week, focus
time, Pomodoro sessions, and your current streak — all from your own
local data.

**Make it yours**
Drag widgets to rearrange them, resize from the corner, hide what you
don't use, and pick an accent color. Optionally sign in with Google to
sync everything to your own Drive — nothing is visible to any other app,
and nothing is ever sent to DevNest's developer.

Works fully offline. No account required.
